*   **Inside-out tracking:** The cameras or sensors are located within the device itself, such as the HMD (lecture 16, 13:26). Because the sensors are attached to or worn by the user, the user can move anywhere while the system continues to work (lecture 16, 18:49).
*   **Outside-in tracking:** The sensors or trackers are external to the device and are placed within the real world or the surrounding environment (lecture 16, 13:26; 16, 15:58). This method restricts the user's movement to the specific area where the external sensors are placed (lecture 16, 13:26; 16, 18:49).
