## L03 [list, expect yes] What are the three fidelities in VR design?
GOLD: Representational fidelity (how far the experience conveys familiarity with a place that is or could be on Earth), interaction fidelity (how far the physical actions for a virtual task match those of the real task), and experiential fidelity (how far the user's personal experience matches the creator's intended experience); developers should keep them high to avoid the uncanny valley.
-- ours_v2 r1: The course describes three types of fidelity in VR:

* **Representational fidelity**: This refers to how well the virtual world resembles the real world, ranging from purely abstract and non-objective worlds (low fidelity) to photorealistic content like 360-degree video (high fidelity; lecture 9, 14:21).
* **Interaction fidelity**: This is the degree to which physical actions for a virtual task correspond to the physical actions for an equivalent real-world task. It can range from no physical motion, such as moving by button press (low fidelity), to intense realistic physical actions (high fidelity; lecture 9, 15:12).
* **Experiential fidelity**: This refers to how closely the virtual experience matches real-world constraints, such as navigation and movement. Low experiential fidelity occurs when a virtual world offers endless possibilities that do not occur in the real world, whereas higher fidelity involves placing real-world constraints on virtual movement and interaction (lecture 9, 16:12).
