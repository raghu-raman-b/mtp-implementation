So, when we are moving any of these body parts, we do it consciously with full knowledge of what we are doing, that awareness is called proprioception or our sixth sense. Kinesthesis, on the other hand, provides information on velocities, acceleration, and forces while moving these body parts. So, proprioception is awareness of movement. Kinesthesis is awareness of various parameters associated with the movement.
Now, in the human somatosensory system, we have at least nine major kinds of receptors, out of which six are fully devoted to touch senses, the sense of touch, and the remaining three are devoted to proprioception and kinesthesis.
