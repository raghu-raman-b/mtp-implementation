Now the third characteristic is user actions partially determine what happens in the environment. What is the implication of this   characteristic? In summary, what we can say is that there are three aspects of VR systems. Artificial environment that is synthetic graphical view of a real or imaginary world. Then   it should be able to generate stimuli so that we are able to experience immersion using our sensory organs. And third is interaction that is dynamic change in the virtual environment based on users actions or rather inputs provided to the environment. So these three are the defining characteristics of any given VR system.
