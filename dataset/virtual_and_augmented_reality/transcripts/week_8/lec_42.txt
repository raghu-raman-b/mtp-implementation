Apart from these three approaches, there is something interesting way of, there is some interesting way of creating prototype called Wizard of Oz approach. It is a very interesting technique and its origin is even more interesting. So, in this approach, a human subject is involved who believes that they are interacting with an autonomous computer or a computer in short.
But in reality, what happens is that the computer is actually operated by another human being who is unseen by the subject. That operator can be called the wizard. So whatever the human subject wants from the computer, the wizard somehow knows and shows in some manner or returns the result in some manner to the subject. The term originated in the early 1980s, although the concept was there beforehand. One very interesting example of this approach is the listening typewriter experiment conducted by IBM way back in 1984. At that time, IBM was trying to develop a speech recognition system and it wanted to test the acceptability of the idea, which required a working system.
The problem was that nobody had a speech recognizer at that time. It was not yet developed and the company did not want to spend money and effort on building a speech recognizer first. Before testing the acceptability of the idea of the listening typewriter. So, the technology was not yet available. However, the company wanted to test the idea first before committing resources to build the technology. So, how to do that? So, they decided to go for a Wizard of Oz experiment. And this is the experimental setup.
There is this human user in front of a microphone. He talks about something. There is a computer. The subject thinks that this is a real computer. However, the computer is connected to another computer here through some intermediary. And there is this wizard sitting hidden away from the subject. This is the subject. This is the wizard. There is a wall. So the subject cannot see the wizard, but the subject is thinking that he is interacting with a computer. So whatever he speaks, the computer displays in the form of text.
So the user says something. It appears on the screen. However, whatever the user says, this microphone is also connected to the speaker of the computer. So, the wizard gets to hear what the subject is speaking, and based on that, the wizard types those characters. Then that gets transmitted because these two computers are connected, and it gets displayed on the subject's computer screen. Effectively letting the subject believe that whatever he has spoken, has appeared on the screen, so this is a good system or this is a bad system, whatever the perception of the subject is.
However, in reality, there is no such technology which can automatically convert that. So, at that time, there was no such technology. So, the human wizard was listening and typing and sending the result back to the subject. So, this setup, this idea is what is called the Wizard of Oz approach.
