The third category of AR devices are the spatial augmented reality devices. So, what are these devices? Let us have a brief understanding. So, these are also known as room-scale AR or tabletop AR. These devices typically use projectors to display graphical information on physical objects.
So, unlike the previous categories where we discussed headsets or glasses or even handheld devices.
