[15/166] Head-up display or HUD can be one such category.
[15/167] These are nowadays quite commonly seen in cars.
[15/168] What happens in these displays is that they project images onto a transparent surface, usually a screen or a visor.
[15/169] So, ideally the surface appears to not be there and only the content is there because the surface is transparent.
[15/170] However, it may not be as transparent as an ideal transparent surface.
