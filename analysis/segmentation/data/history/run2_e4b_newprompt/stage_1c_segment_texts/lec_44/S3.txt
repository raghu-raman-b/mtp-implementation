[44/39] Already, there are well-known questionnaires. available which can be directly used.
[44/40] So, for post-task data collection we have post-task questionnaire such as After Scenario Questionnaire or ASQ designed way back in 1991 by Lewis.
[44/41] Then Expectation Measure or EM questionnaire designed in 2003 by Albert and Dixon.
[44/42] Similarly, for post-session data collection, we have post-session questionnaires such as System Usability Scale or SUS, a very popular questionnaire designed in 1996 by Brooke.
[44/43] Then Computer System Usability Questionnaire CSUQ, again designed by Lewis in 1995.
[44/44] Questionnaire for User Interface Satisfaction QUIS, designed by Chin et al., in 1988 that is even before then Usefulness Satisfaction and Ease of Use questionnaire or USE proposed by Lund in 2001.
[44/45] So, as you can see there are different questionnaire sets meant for collecting data at different points of time.
