[14/158] These are hardware devices that move the entire body resulting in a sense of physical motion and gravity.
[14/159] It conveys a sense of orientation, vibration, acceleration, and jerks.
[14/160] However, use of such platforms may result in motion sickness. which can be reduced by decreasing conflict between visual motion and the perceived motion in the real world.
[14/161] So, such motion platforms can be of two types: active motion platform.
[14/162] These are controlled by computer simulation as shown here.
[14/163] So, computer controls the platform whereas, there can be passive motion platform which are controlled by the user; there is no direct computer control here.
