[8/58] What does immersion mean?
[8/59] Like we have done before while defining virtual reality, let us similarly begin with a definition of immersion.
[8/60] It is not a dictionary definition; rather, this is a definition taken from the literature which says: Immersion is the objective degree to which a VR system and application projects stimuli onto the sensory receptors of users in a way that is extensive, matching, surrounding, vivid, interactive and plot informing.
[8/61] So, what does it say?
[8/62] It says that immersion is the objective degree.
[8/63] A degree of what?
[8/64] Objective degree to which a VR system and application projects stimuli onto our or the human user's sensory receptors in a way such that The experience is extensive, matching, surrounding, vivid, interactive and plot informing.
[8/65] This is a definition which was originally proposed by Slater and Wilbur.
[8/66] I will give you the reference at the end of this lecture.
[8/67] So, the terms to note in the definition are objective degree, VR system and application, projects stimuli, sensory receptors of users and of course, the seven terms namely extensive, matching, surrounding, vivid, interactive and plot informing, these six terms.
[8/68] So, in a nutshell, we can say that immersion is the objective degree which decides the qualities of experience, That a user is likely to have while dealing with a VR system.
[8/69] And these qualities are encapsulated in terms of these six terms: extensive, matching, surrounding, vivid, interactive and plot informing.
