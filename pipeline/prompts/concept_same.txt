TASK
You decide which entries of a course's concept list are the same concept. The entries below were collected from different segments and lectures of one course, and they have similar names or meanings. Each entry has a name, the other names used for it, the lectures it comes from, and facts from those lectures that name it. Sort the entries into groups: entries that are the same concept go into one group, and an entry that is the same as no other entry is a group of its own.

{course}

SAME OR DIFFERENT
- Two entries are the same concept when they are one thing of the subject under different names or wordings: a full name and its acronym, a spelling or singular and plural variant, a synonym ("headset" and "head-mounted display"), or the same name collected in two lectures. Their facts describe the same thing, and a student who looks up either name should find one glossary entry with the facts of both.
- Decide from the facts, not from the names alone. The same name can mean different things in different lectures (the "resolution" of a display and the "resolution" of a tracker), and different names can mean one thing.
- Two entries are different concepts when one is a kind of the other ("display" and "head-mounted display"), a part of the other ("lens" and "head-mounted display"), a property, measure or use of the other ("tracking" and "tracking accuracy"), the opposite of the other, or a related thing that is often named with it ("augmented reality" and "mixed reality").
- When the facts are too few to tell, keep the entries apart. A wrong merge mixes the facts of two concepts; a missed merge only leaves two entries.

THE ENTRIES
Each entry is given with its number, its name, its other names, its lectures, and its facts. Facts marked * come from a segment that defines the concept.

{entries}

ANSWER
Answer with a JSON object with one field, "groups": a list of groups. Every entry number appears in exactly one group. Each group has these fields in this order:
"entries": the numbers of the entries in the group.
"reason": one sentence. For a group of several entries, what in their facts shows that they are the same concept. For a group of one, what makes it different from the entry closest to it.
"name": the name of the group's concept, chosen from the names of its entries: full words, singular, the wording the course uses most. For a group of one, its own name.
Answer with the JSON only.
