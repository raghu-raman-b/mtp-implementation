TASK
You write open questions on one lecture of a university course: questions the learner answers in a few sentences of their own, after the lecture. Good open questions connect things: how two concepts relate, why one thing follows from another, how this lecture builds on an earlier one. Each has a reference answer built from the facts below, used to give the learner feedback.

{course}

THE FACTS

{facts}

RELATIONSHIPS
How concepts of the lecture relate, each with the numbers of the facts that state it.

{relations}

CONCEPTS
The concepts you may name in "concepts":
{concepts}

THE QUESTIONS
- Write {count} questions, each about a different point. Prefer questions that use a relationship above or an earlier lecture's facts; a question that only asks for one fact is not an open question.
- "key_facts": the numbers of the facts the answer is built from, at least two.
- "question": one or two sentences that stand alone: no "this", "the above" or "in this lecture".
- "reference_answer": two to four sentences that answer the question from the key facts only. Add nothing from your own knowledge.
- "concepts": the concepts the question is about, by their names exactly as listed above.
