TASK
You check facts extracted from one segment of a university lecture transcript. A fact is a single teachable proposition about the course subject that the lecturer establishes in the segment. Check each fact against the seven properties below and decide whether it has all of them.

{course}

THIS LECTURE
This lecture is titled "{lecture_title}".
The goal of the segment: {subgoal}

THE SEVEN PROPERTIES
1. Established. The lecturer asserts it as true. Not a hypothesis, a question, an opinion or view, or something the lecturer only asks about.
2. Grounded. Its evidence sentences state it: every part of the fact is found in them.
3. Self-contained. A reader who has not seen the lecture understands it. No pronouns or pointing words (this, it, here, that method, the slide) that the fact itself does not make clear. No "the lecturer says".
4. Atomic. One proposition: it can be tested on its own and cannot be split into smaller facts. It keeps the conditions, qualifiers, quantities and scope that make it true. One definition, or one list of members, is one fact.
5. On-subject. It is about the goal of the segment and the course subject. Not a greeting, logistics, an anecdote, a joke, a rhetorical question, or a promise of later coverage ("we will see this next week").
6. Canonical. Every term is written in full words, never as an acronym or abbreviation: "extended reality", not "XR" or "extended reality (XR)".
7. Faithful. It adds nothing the lecturer did not say, even if it is true. It keeps the lecturer's hedges ("usually", "in most cases"). If the lecturer corrects themselves, it keeps only the corrected version.

A fact passes only if it has all seven properties.

THE SEGMENT
One sentence per line, each with its number in brackets.

{segment}

THE FACTS
Each fact is given with its ID, the numbers of the segment sentences it cites as evidence, and its text.

{facts}

ANSWER

"fact": the fact's ID.
"reason": one or two sentences. If the fact fails, which properties it fails and why; if it passes, why it has all seven.
"pass": true or false.
Answer with the JSON only.
