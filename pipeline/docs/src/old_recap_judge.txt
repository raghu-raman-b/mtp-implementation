THE CRITERIA
For each point and for the text:
1. supported. Everything it says is stated by the facts: for a point, by the facts it cites; for the text, by the facts of the segment. It fails when it adds anything from outside, even something true, or when the support is in a fact the point does not cite (give the number of the fact to add).
2. faithful. A learner who remembers it believes what the facts say, no more and no less. It fails when a term is swapped for another one ("mass" turned into "weight"), when a hedge or a condition is dropped ("can", "usually", "if the list is already sorted"), when one side of a comparison is lost, or when the statement is made stronger, weaker or wider than the fact. For the text it also fails when it joins two ideas with a cause or a purpose that neither the facts nor the lines under THE SEGMENT give.
