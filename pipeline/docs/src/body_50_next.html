Upcoming fixes

Done 2026-11-18

Display rules from the pipeline's signals

Recaps show only judge-passing points; crosswords show failed words filled in, without their clues; connecting concepts have no card and give question answering their facts only; formulas typeset with KaTeX; the model and embedder warmed at start (What fails where).

Not fixed

Duplicates and odd aliases

HMD / VR headset, interaction / user interaction, iOS / iPhone OS, FOV on two concepts, aliases such as “present” and “system”. Code finds them, but no pipeline signal says which is right, and no stronger model or hand-made list may decide: future work for the pipeline itself.

Viewer

The redesign

Being done by another agent as of 2026-11-17.

Deployment

Remote-model setup: built, rehearsal next

Only the model and embedder on the GPU server; the viewer, layer, videos and tool loop on the learner's machine (Running the demo). Not yet run on a real instance or on Windows.

Done 2026-11-19

Baseline experiments

Plain LLM, RAG (with and without thinking), GraphRAG (with and without text) and two ablations on 65 questions, judged for correctness, false claims, citations, “not covered” and time (What the method buys). The activities study is still running.

Question answering

One tool call instead of several rounds

Fetch every concept and fact a question needs in one tool call, so an answer takes about one tool call before it starts, like RAG's one retrieval (point 4). Planned, not built or measured.

Open for revision

Course-structure statements

Fact extraction leaves out what the course says about itself (rule 7), so “What are the six modules?” fails where RAG answers it (point 5). Whether to change it is not decided.

Future work: what can be improved further

IdeaWhy, and what in the repository points to it
Finish the planned gatesThe block tests of PLAN.md were deferred by the MVP: the fact-judge rerun with the revised prompts (block 1), concept gold (block 2), pair gold (block 3), the scored question set (block 6, draft of 85 questions on a branch).
A judge for every kind, measured firstThe activity judge runs on six kinds and is not yet measured on its seed set; recall, match the pairs and complete the map have no judge. The fact judge needs majority voting or another fix for its 15% flips.
A smaller or distilled answer modelThe answer time is mostly model time (round 0 about 1.6 s, the final answer about 2.6 s at about 60 tokens per second). A smaller model, or one tuned on the course's tool calls, would cut it; tool choice by a small model is the known risk (B17).
Faster decodingAn FP8 Gemma checkpoint on vLLM would roughly double decode speed over BF16 (analysis/qa/STATE.md); speculative decoding is untried; a forced first tool call works on vLLM but needs a guard (it skipped the search on B17).
Batch the whole build1a, 1b, 2a and 2c ran at 32 parallel requests; 4a reached about 1,800 tokens per second at 128. Running every stage at 128 on one GPU, and stages whose inputs are ready side by side, would shorten the 10.8 hours.
Incremental updatesThe cache already skips unchanged requests. A lecture that changes reruns its own segments, facts and concepts; the registry, definitions of the concepts it touches and the graph would then need a targeted rerun rather than a full one.
Relationships across segmentsD4: a later pass over concept pairs that share a lecture but never a segment.
Cross-course concept linkingStage 0a already runs on six courses (VR and five others); one registry over several courses would let a learner follow a concept from one course to another.
Learner modellingReview cards are scheduled per learner in the browser only; quiz answers could feed the schedule and the recall before a lecture.
Multilingual subtitlesThe pipeline trusts human punctuation (0a); other languages need their own splitting rules and prompts in that language or translation of the layer.
Evaluation at scaleMore than one human annotator for segmentation, blind gold for facts and concepts, the question set scored automatically, and the baseline tests repeated on another course and with the 27B model.
