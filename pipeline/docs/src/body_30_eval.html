Question answering

The bench (analysis/qa/bench.py): 17 questions chosen to hit the weak points, run in six variants. Each cell is the time of each run and whether the answer said the course covers it. What fails where: comparisons are the slowest (two cards, long answers); turning thinking off makes the model skip the search and wrongly answer B17 “not covered”; B01–B03 are partly taught topics, answered “not covered as such” with the closest facts.
