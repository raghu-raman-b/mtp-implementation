The second issue is we have two points for data collection. After every task, so we are assuming that the users are given some tasks, to perform on the prototype, and then we are collecting feedback. Now, there can be two data collection points. One is after every task is performed, and the second is at the end of the session where all the tasks are performed. So, when should we collect the data? Of course, it is desirable to collect data at both points if feasible. Otherwise, we have to choose either of these two points.
