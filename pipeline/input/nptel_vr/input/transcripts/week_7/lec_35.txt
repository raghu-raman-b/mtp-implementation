So, as to understand how the usability requirements are gathered and subsequently how those are used, analyzed, and used. Now, the contextual inquiry method is an old method. Its importance in user-centered design was highlighted way back in 1988, although the term used at that time was phenomenological research method. So, it is more than 30 years old. Now, this method is often referred to as a semi-structured interview process, which consists of both interviews and observations. Now, what is the primary objective of this method of contextual inquiry? The primary objective is to watch and observe users perform tasks in their natural work setting or, in other words, the work context. So, when you say usability is the quality when it is measured against a specified context of use, in other words, the work context. So, contextual inquiry essentially tries to gather end user requirements when the users are working in a specified context, which we can call a natural work setting.
Now, during observations, when we are following the contextual inquiry method, the users may be asked questions to clarify certain behaviors or activities. So, this is the part of the interview. That is asking for clarification. However, the primary objective is to observe and to gather more insight about user behavior. The method also provides for interacting with the users for clarification. Generally, it takes one to two hours if everything is well planned. Now, the observations including interaction with the users are supposed to enable the designers to learn about the task domain, work culture and physical and social constraints at the workplace based on which design decisions can be made.
So, the knowledge is important since the specific work context is an essential part of the definition of usability. So, since usability is defined in a specific work context, it is essential to know how the users behave in that specific work context to ensure that the software or the product or the system that we are building is usable.
